Designers and art directors now treat generative models as an infinite mood-board and concept engine. The workflow typically looks like:

1. Prompt + reference exploration for direction
2. Selection and refinement of strongest directions
3. Upscaling, inpainting, and control-net guided iterations
4. Export of high-resolution assets or 3D-ready concepts

Because iteration is cheap and nearly instantaneous, teams explore far more visual territory before committing to a final direction. The bottleneck has moved from “can we generate options?” to “which options deserve human polish and production resources?”
