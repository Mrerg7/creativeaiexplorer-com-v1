Music generation models have matured past novelty. In 2026 professional and semi-pro producers use them as:

- **Idea engines** — rapid genre and mood sketches
- **Arrangement partners** — expanding a core melody into full instrumentation
- **Stem generators** — producing usable stems for further human production in a DAW
- **Vocal and harmony assistants**

The strongest results still come from hybrid workflows: human composition and taste + AI expansion + traditional mixing and mastering. The models remove the “blank page” friction and the time cost of rough demos, letting creators spend more energy on emotional impact and originality.
